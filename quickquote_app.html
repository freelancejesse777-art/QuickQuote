Who to send it to
Best fits: solo operators or small 2–5 person crews in trades with variable, describe-the-job pricing — HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, pest control. Skip large companies (they already have office software) and businesses doing under ~2 quotes a week (not enough volume to feel the pain).

Best channels for small/local markets specifically: people you already know in the trades (strongest), local Facebook groups and Nextdoor (often more active than Reddit for small towns), direct text/email to businesses you find above. Reddit works better for larger metro areas — check r/[your city] or r/[your trade] to see if there's real activity before relying on it.
